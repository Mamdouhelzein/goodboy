# Csv Cleaning

Clean messy CSV or tabular data with pandas: fix headers, types, missing values, duplicates and encodings, and export clean data.

## Load

pd.read_csv with encoding, sep and dtype options; inspect with df.info().

## Clean

Strip whitespace, parse dates, drop duplicates, handle NaN explicitly.

## Export

Write back to CSV or parquet.
