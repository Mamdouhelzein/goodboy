# Ctf Crypto

Solve CTF cryptography challenges: Caesar and Vigenere ciphers, XOR, base64 and hex encodings, hash cracking, and weak RSA.

## Encodings

Recognize base64, hex, base32 and URL encoding; CyberChef helps.

## Classical

Brute force Caesar shifts, frequency analysis for substitution.

## RSA

Small e, common modulus, factor small n.
